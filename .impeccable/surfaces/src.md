# NURI storefront + admin — surface brief

Scope: storefront (Persuade, mobile-first, Arabic RTL default / English LTR) and admin (Operate, desktop-first). One world, two registers.

Audience/job: Egyptian and Gulf shoppers choosing authentic Korean and imported skincare by skin type and routine step; the owner running orders, COD, batches/expiry, stock, customers, discounts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Light through a lattice. Korean changsal window panes and Arab mashrabiya both filter light through a wooden grid; NURI ("my light" / "the world") sells skin that glows. The page is a lacquered lattice holding panes of lit paper, products standing behind them. Refuses the category default of pastel-pink glass-skin bubbles and its opposite, black-and-gold luxury.

OWN-WORLD: Ottchil lacquer oxblood owns hero, nav, footer and admin rail (committed, 30–50%). Panes are lit hanji in a cool celadon-white, never cream. Brass is the single live light (one at a time). Celadon green is the quiet second ink. Muntins: 1px–2px lacquer lines, panes square-cornered inside, 14px outer radius only on the frame. Flat inks, no gradients except the light itself. Reem Kufi (square Kufic, Arabic + Latin) for display; Readex Pro for text and tabular numerals.

STORY: Visitor sees one lit window of products, learns these are authentic, skin-matched Korean and imported formulas, picks a routine step or skin type, buys with COD in two taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed lacquer field. A large lattice window (ah-ja pattern meeting a girih star at its center) spans ~60% width at the start side; behind its lit panes stand 5 product silhouettes. The light source follows the pointer (scroll on touch) and casts lattice shadow across the headline at the end side. Headline in Reem Kufi at ~5.5rem; one line of support; primary action "تسوقي روتينك" in brass; secondary "اعرفي نوع بشرتك". Morning/evening light switch (AM/PM routine) sits on the window sill.

FORM: Grounded candidate 7 of 7 (changsal × mashrabiya lattice light). Seed key f37fb1f4. Raises — from oscilloscope: every element snaps to the lattice module (8px muntin grid), dashboard charts drawn on the same graticule. From rain-night city: light state follows the real hour (AM routine light vs PM routine dusk). From WPA poster: one imperative per section, flat inks only. From neo-tokyo: exactly one brass light is live at a time. Competitive alternate kept: provenance ribbon → product pages show a batch provenance trail (Seoul lab → import shipment → Cairo shelf → expiry).

Signature interaction: pointer-driven light casting the lattice shadow; sliding-panel (hanok door) reveal for quick view and cart drawer.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

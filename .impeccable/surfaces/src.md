# NURI storefront + admin — surface brief

Scope: storefront (Persuade, mobile-first, Arabic RTL default / English LTR) and admin (Operate). One brand, two registers.

Audience/job: Egyptian and Gulf shoppers choosing authentic Korean and imported skincare by skin type and routine step; the owner running the store.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The owner pinned the reference (vibebevvy.com): "same design, same entrance, everything". Joyful, loud, confident DTC beauty: big laughing close-up photography, a chunky soft serif shouting short lines, a lilac ticker, a lemon pill CTA. Refuses the hushed pastel K-beauty catalogue. The previous lattice-light world is discarded (owner: the design was bad).

OWN-WORLD: Cream ground #FFF1E7, plum ink #1F0229 for all type and 1px hairlines, lilac #DB98F9 brand colour (ticker bar, wordmark, stickers), lemon #FFFC54 only for the elliptical pill CTA with a plum 1px outline and arrow. Fraunces (heavy, SOFT 100) for Latin display, Lalezar for Arabic display, Space Mono for Latin small print, Readex Pro for Arabic text. Product grid = hairline-ruled cells, no shadows. Photos in 16px-radius frames.

STORY: First viewport makes them smile and want the glow; the grid sells the bestsellers; the accordion answers "why NURI" (authentic lots, skin fit, COD); reviews prove it; routines and "seen in the wild" make it social; the footer says the line again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Lilac ticker bar (authentic, COD, free shipping, lot & expiry). Transparent header over the photo: links at the inline-start, NURI wordmark centred in lilac, round outlined icon buttons at the inline-end. Full-bleed hero photo (laughing woman, cream on her cheek). Headline "بشرتك، بس أحلى." / "Glow, cultured in Seoul." drops in letter by letter (staggered bounce) at the start side, bottom-third; support line under it; lemon pill "تسوقي دلوقتي" / "Shop now" centred near the bottom. On scroll the hero shrinks into an inset rounded card.

FORM: Owner-pinned reference (overrides roll f37fb1f4). Signature interactions: letter-drop intro + hero shrink-to-card on scroll; giant "NURI نوري" word band with a product spinning on scroll; scroll-scrubbed heading fill; endless marquees.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
